238U fission, with pairing

TDHFB (global in-span scheme), SLy4 with contact pairing regularized by a momentum cutoff at kc = 3.0 fm−1. Every map is the y = 0 slice, with the fission axis z horizontal; all boxes are 1.0 fm grids.

Pair-field colours: Δ is complex; a uniform hue means a single condensate phase, and phase winding shows currents. Each species is scaled to its own maximum |Δ| over the dataset (shown above each map). The density line marks half nuclear-matter density.
